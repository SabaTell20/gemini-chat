{name} only uses cookies that are **strictly necessary** to sign you in and keep the site secure. We use **no** analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, so we don't need to ask for consent with a banner.

## Cookies we use

| Name | Set by | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gemini_chat_session` | Gemini Chat | Keeps you signed in. It holds a random key that page scripts can't read; only a scrambled (hashed) copy is stored on our side. | Until you close the browser, or 30 days if you tick "Keep me signed in". Logging out ends it. |

## Session storage

| Name | Set by | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `chat_id`, `chat_options` | Gemini Chat | Remembers which chat is open and which options are on while you move between pages | Until you close the tab |

## Other services

The voice screen connects to **ElevenLabs**, which can see your IP address, but it does not set cookies through this site. The fonts are served by the app itself. See the [Privacy Policy](/Privacy).

## Managing cookies

You can delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. If you delete `gemini_chat_session`, you will simply have to sign in again.
